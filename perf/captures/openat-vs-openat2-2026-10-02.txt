Child-directory open experiment
Date: 2026-10-02
Base revision: bd507f7 (release build)
Candidate: same source with child-directory open changed from openat2(resolve=0) plus openat fallback to openat
Host: Linux 6.18.33-0-lts, ext4 on /dev/nvme0n1p3
Workload: perf/fixture, generated inode-heavy tree; four Rayon workers

Correctness
- Allocated stdout and stderr matched byte-for-byte.
- Apparent stdout and stderr matched byte-for-byte.

One-scan filesystem syscall counts (base -> candidate)
- statx:       1,218,248 -> 1,218,248
- getdents64:     85,777 -> 85,777
- close:          85,784 -> 85,784
- clone:               4 -> 4
- openat2:       85,772 -> 0
- openat:             0 -> 85,772

Two alternating pairs of 39-scan release batches (base, candidate; then candidate, base)
- Pair A elapsed: 9.644 s, 10.025 s
- Pair B elapsed: 9.466 s, 10.158 s
- Mean user cycles: 6.100 billion, 6.218 billion (+1.94% candidate)
- Mean user instructions: 15.844 billion, 15.934 billion (+0.57% candidate)
- Mean elapsed: 9.555 s, 10.092 s (+5.61% candidate)

This small experiment favored keeping openat2. Timing is sensitive to host load; both observed pairs were slower with openat, while syscall totals other than the open call name were unchanged.
